Mono, where ink is the only brightness there is

The heaviest ASCII glyph in this raster inks 26.2% of its cell, so a bright image clips every cell to @ and the ramp stops carrying anything. Normalising stretches the source onto the range the alphabet can actually express. SSIM stays low either way, because white ink on black cannot be as bright as the picture; the per-cell correlation is what moves.
